SFB Value

Privacy

SFB Value is the donation valuation tool of the Strongsville Emergency Food Bank. It has no accounts, no sign-in, no advertising and no analytics.

What stays on the phone

The price list, the donation tally, Recent items, settings and the barcode matches remembered on the phone are stored in the browser's storage on the phone itself. Reset app data in Settings removes them.

What leaves the phone

  • Price list downloads and app updates: ordinary web requests to the app's own address.
  • Live price check (can be turned off in Settings): when the phone is online, the barcode of a scanned or typed item is sent to the food bank's price service, which asks Walmart for the current price. Only the barcode is sent.
  • Shared barcode matches: when a volunteer matches an unknown barcode to a Walmart item by searching for it, the pair (barcode, Walmart item) is shared with the food bank's other phones through the same service, so the next volunteer sees it at once. No name, device or location is attached.

Camera

The camera is used only to read barcodes, on the phone. No image is stored or sent anywhere. On an iPhone the camera stays on, out of sight, for up to four hours after the last scan while the app is open, so the phone does not ask for camera access again during a shift; no picture is read during that time, and the phone's camera indicator shows it. Closing the app ends it.
